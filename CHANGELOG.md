# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Step-by-step waypoints for 33 Midnight exploration achievements: treasures, lore objects, telescopes, NPC hunts, Arcantina collections and each zone's rares.
- Hover a step for how to actually find it (hidden notes, caves, ledges, puzzle steps); steps with instructions show a [?].
- `/mach` window lists what's left, hides finished steps, and has filters for completed achievements and your current zone.
- Click a step to set a map pin (TomTom if installed, otherwise the built-in waypoint). Dungeon and raid steps pin the instance entrance.
- "Nearest step" finds the closest unfinished step on your continent, and the pin moves to the next step automatically when you finish one.
- Steps that must be done in order stay locked until the previous one is done.
- Moth button in Harandar: pins the nearest collectable Glowing Moth and can switch on soft interact so your Interact key catches it. Moths above your Hara'ti renown are shown locked.
- Chronicler of the Haranir lore objects stay locked until you're on their legend's weekly quest.
- Ctrl-click a step for its Wowhead link; Alt-click to mark a step done by hand if it doesn't tick off.
- `/mach check` compares the addon's data with the game; `/mach snap` / `/mach diff` show which hidden quests an action flags.
